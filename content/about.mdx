I'm a third-year computer science engineering student in Ahmedabad, India, graduating in 2028. I completed the NextLeap Product Management Fellowship (Cohort 52).

I work like a technical PM. When I have a product question I build the prototype myself and test my thinking against real data: 13,000 app reviews, a hand-labelled answer key, interviews I ran myself. I'd rather publish a result with its weak points listed than a clean story I can't defend.

The three projects under [Work](/work) are each written up as a case study: a short account of one project that covers the problem, what I did, the decisions and tradeoffs, what I found and what I'd do differently. Each one also states plainly what AI did and what I did. They weren't planned as a set: each grew out of a question the previous one left me with, and the Work page explains how.

The code for all of them is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Sam-732), and I'm on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samprat-trivedi-b01098300/). I'm open to product management internships.
