These three projects weren't planned as a set. Each one started with a question the previous one left me with.

It began with Wispr Flow, an app I use to dictate instead of type. I liked it, and wondered why more people don't use ChatGPT's voice mode. I assumed they couldn't find it. My research showed something else: many people felt awkward talking to their phone around others.

Interviewing people for that research, I got more interested in their frustrations than in voice itself. Product teams get thousands of reviews and complaints, so how do they make sense of them? That became VoC Copilot.

VoC Copilot used AI to sort the reviews, which raised the next question: how much should a team trust what AI tells it? GrowPulse, a project for my NextLeap cohort, was my answer. The model only sorts; code owns every number, quote and link.
